Core services

WMAS ENGINEERING LTD. provides CAD and mechanical design support for engineering, fabrication and manufacturing projects. Services are built around practical delivery, clear technical output and dependable support for real production environments

Mechanical Design and CAD Modelling

Creation and development of clean 3D models for fabricated structures, plant support equipment, access systems and mechanical assemblies

Fabrication Drawings and Detailing

Production of clear drawing packs to support manufacture, assembly and installation, with attention to revision control, usability and fabrication flow

Steelwork, Access Platforms and Stair Systems

Design support for access and egress structures including platforms, walkways, landings, staircases, guardrails and associated steelwork

Equipment and Plant Layouts

Layout modelling and spatial coordination for equipment, plant areas, automation cells and constrained site conditions

Legacy Model Updates and 2D to 3D Conversion

Support for updating older models, cleaning CAD data and converting existing information into structured 3D output

Additional Design Capacity

Flexible support for businesses that need reliable CAD and drawing output during peak workload periods or for self contained project packages
