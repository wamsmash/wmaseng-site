About WMAS ENGINEERING LTD.

WMAS ENGINEERING LTD. provides precision CAD and mechanical design support for engineering, fabrication and manufacturing teams working across steelwork, access systems, plant layouts and equipment integration

The business supports projects that require clear modelling, practical thinking and fabrication ready output. This includes access platforms, stair and walkway systems, mechanical assemblies, conveyor related structures, legacy model updates and drawing packages for manufacture

The approach is grounded in real project delivery. That means understanding constraints, maintaining control of revisions, working clearly within established standards and producing design information that can be used with confidence by fabricators and engineering teams

WMAS ENGINEERING LTD. is built around dependable support rather than noise. The priority is to deliver clean CAD work, clear drawings and useful technical output that helps projects move forward efficiently

Built around real project conditions

Whether the requirement is a one off package or additional support during a busy period, the objective stays the same, accurate work, practical communication and reliable turnaround

The work is intended to support engineers, fabricators and project managers with information that can move cleanly into manufacture, installation and handover
